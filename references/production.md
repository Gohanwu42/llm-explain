# Production investment

Use this reference only for diagram, HTML, or video production. The selected level controls customization and refinement, not factual accuracy or explanation depth. User-supplied scope and explicit waivers take precedence.

| Boundary | Lightweight | Polished |
|---|---|---|
| Approach | Reuse suitable components; minimize production overhead. | Customize visual hierarchy and expression around the actual content. |
| Preview | Produce directly. | Show one representative preview using real content, then wait for direction approval. |
| Direction refinement | No mandatory direction review. | At most one preview revision before full production. |
| Full production | One complete direction. | One complete direction after approval. |
| Repair | At most two local repair-and-recheck cycles after initial verification. | Same. |

Choose diagram count, interactions, and duration from the learning goal. A short artifact can be polished; a lightweight artifact can explain a deep idea. Style references are optional, not an extra required form or a fixed menu.

## Preflight and boundaries

1. Confirm the selected production path can produce, inspect, and deliver the requested medium with available tools. Respect the host's media-tool instructions.
2. Separate known token use, media charges, and elapsed time. Do not invent estimates or equate the production level with a spending authorization.
3. Honor stated spending limits. Confirm an actual added paid service or budget increase before incurring it.
4. Prefer portable resources and licensed assets. Inspect a borrowed template's assumptions, including aspect ratio and dependencies.

A preview demonstrates the key relationship: a representative diagram, working HTML interaction, or short narrated video segment. A mood-board name or script alone is not that preview. If preview production is unavailable, describe the limitation and let the learner choose a feasible alternative.

If a critical error remains after the repair allowance, retain the work and report the issue and its impact. Ask whether to invest more or switch approach. Do not label the artifact verified. A direction change or complete regeneration is additional production, not a local repair.

## Delivery

Provide the viewable/playable artifact, plus available reusable sources. State only material verification results and limitations. Separate fact checks, artifact operation, and the learner's response. Test fixtures and development logs do not belong in the learner's explanation.
