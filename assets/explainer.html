Every minute adds 1 L. After 10 min, the tank has gained 10 L.

Where this model works
  • Each flow stays constant. Capacity stays fixed during a run. Other gains and losses are ignored.
  • The calculation ends when the tank first reaches full or empty. What happens next needs another model.
  • Liters measure the amount. Liters per minute measure a rate. Multiplying a rate by minutes gives an amount.
  • The equation keeps the input precision. The ≈ sign marks an approximate result.
