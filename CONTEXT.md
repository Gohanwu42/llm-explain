# LLM Explain

A reusable explanation skill that helps a learner understand a concept through a chosen medium and an explicit production investment.

## Language

**Explanation run**:
One learning request with one primary medium and its selected settings. Clarification, local repair, and feedback on that explanation belong to the same run; a new learning task or a change of primary medium starts a new run.

**Learning objective**:
The relationship, mechanism, or decision the learner needs to explain and apply. It determines necessary content depth independently of production investment.

**Primary medium**:
The learner's selected main deliverable: text, diagram, interactive HTML, or video. Supporting text and labels serve that deliverable.

**STE-inspired strength**:
A learner-selected value from 0% to 100% controlling the strength of clear-language constraints inspired by ASD-STE100. It is a style instruction, not a measured compliance rate or a content-retention percentage.
_Avoid_: STE compliance score, knowledge compression ratio

**Production level**:
The selected investment in customization, media production, and refinement: lightweight or polished. Both levels have the same correctness and usability requirements.
_Avoid_: content depth, correctness level

**Production preview**:
A small, representative part of the intended artifact using real task content. It lets the learner approve a polished production's direction before full production.

**Artifact verification**:
Checks of factual relationships, calculations, rendering, and operation that were actually performed on the deliverable. It does not establish learner understanding.

**Understanding check**:
One optional task that asks the learner to explain a relationship or apply it to a new example. Its result is evidence about that response, not proof of durable mastery.
